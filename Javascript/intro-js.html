Forest

Forests are home to a wide variety of animals, including mammals, birds, insects, and reptiles. They provide shelter, food, and breeding grounds for countless species.











Ocean

The ocean is teeming with life, from tiny plankton to massive whales. Coral reefs are particularly rich ecosystems.











Desert

Deserts may seem barren, but they are home to unique animals like camels, snakes, and lizards that have adapted to the harsh conditions.

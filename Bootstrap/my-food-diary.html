My Food Diary

Accross SEQ

By Mudit Gupta

SEQ is a region in Queensland, Australia, very well known for its diverse cuisine and vibrant food scene.

SEQ specialises in Asian food and has the best asian food in the country due to the mass migration from asia in the '70s and '80s

SEQ is home to a vibrant food culture that reflects its rich history and diverse population. Ranging from traditional local dishes to international cuisine, the region offers a unique culinary experience.

Top 10 dishes in SEQ

  1. Dumplings
  2. Pho
  3. Pad Thai
  4. Korean BBQ
  5. Dim Sum
  6. Sushi
  7. Spring Rolls
  8. Hot Pot
  9. Ramen
  10. Satay

There are also lots of places to get asian food

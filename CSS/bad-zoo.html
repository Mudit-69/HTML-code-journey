Lion


The lion is a large cat of the genus Panthera native to Africa and India. It has a muscular, deep-chested body, short, rounded head, round ears, and a hairy tuft at the end of its tail. It is sexually dimorphic;>

cool fact

Lions are the national animal of several countries, including India and Belgium.




Tiger


The tiger is the largest cat species, native to Asia. It is a carnivorous mammal of the genus Panthera. Tigers are apex predators, primarily hunting deer, wild boar, and other large ungulates.


cool fact

Tigers are excellent swimmers and often hunt in water.




Bear


The bear is a large mammal of the family Ursidae. Bears are found in a variety of habitats, including forests, mountains, and tundra. They are omnivorous, with diets that can include plants, insects, and small mammals.


cool fact

Bears have an excellent sense of smell, which they use to locate food and detect danger.

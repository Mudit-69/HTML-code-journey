All About Spider Man


Spider Man is a fictional superhero created by writer Stan Lee and writer-artist Steve Ditko. He first appeared in Amazing Fantasy #15 in 1962. Peter Parker, a high school student, gains spider-like abilities after being bitten by a radioactive spider. He uses his powers to fight crime and protect the city of New York. Spider-Man is known for his web-slinging abilities and his ability to cling to walls and ceilings.



Spiderman Powers


Spider-Man has several superhuman abilities, including enhanced strength, agility, and reflexes. He can cling to walls and ceilings, and his spider-sense alerts him to danger. He also has the ability to shoot webs from his wrists.



Spiderman Villains


Spider-Man faces numerous villains in his adventures, including the Green Goblin, Doctor Octopus, Venom, and the Kingpin. These antagonists often have their own motivations and powers that challenge Spider-Man's abilities.

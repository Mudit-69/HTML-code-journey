Timeline
Term 1 · Sat arvo

Shifted from Saturday morning to afternoon badminton. Met Mia and Lynne as a “side product” of a bad change.

Prime Era

10‑hour calls, midnight chats, healthy conversations, gossip, trust. They listened to everything and gave opinions.

Love Problems

Mia trusted me with Jayden. Lynne trusted me enough to watch Obsession together. I felt included and needed.

Boundary Crossed

Clipped things I shouldn’t have, didn’t delete when asked, edits and clips made them uncomfortable. Trust broke.

Block & Distance

Lynne blocked me everywhere. Mia blocked my main but not my alt. The prime ended; grief and guilt started.

What they were to me

I didn’t just lose “two people”. I lost:

  • a space where I felt included
  • a safe vent for gossip
  • a homegirl
  • a girl’s number
  • someone who trusted me with their problems
“Mia trusted me enough to show her problems and personal stuff, I betrayed her. That’s my regret.”
